Features

Built around how prerequisites actually works

Three things the system does before you ever queue at the registrar.

Prerequisite verification

Every subject in your prospectus is checked against your academic record before enrolment — so nothing gets rejected at the counter.

Explained results

When a subject is locked, you see the exact reason — which prerequisite is missing, which year standing is required, and what to take first.

Term recommendations

A suggested subject load for the coming term that respects unit limits, sequencing, and what you still need to graduate on time.

How it works

We bring the pieces together so prerequisites stops being guesswork

  1. 1

    Your record goes in

    The registrar uploads grades, or you check what's already on file. Subjects taken, passed, failed, and in progress are all read.

  2. 2

    Rules are matched against the prospectus

    Every prerequisite chain, year standing, and elective group in the BSIT curriculum is checked — subject by subject.

  3. 3

    You get a result and a reason

    Eligible, locked, or already taken — with the specific requirement behind every locked subject spelled out.

  4. 4

    Prerequisites gets easier

    Bring a load you already know will clear. Faculty and registrar review the same result you're looking at.
