How registration works

Accounts are not created instantly. Every request is checked against university records before access is granted.

  1. 1

    You submit a request

    New students provide their details. Students with an existing record only need their student ID.

  2. 2

    The Registrar verifies it

    Your details are matched against the official student list. Your program, year level, and prospectus are set from institutional records.

  3. 3

    You are notified

    Once verified, you can sign in and see your subject eligibility for the coming term.

Use your official university email. Requests that cannot be matched to a student record are declined.
