About CurricuLogic

A prerequisite verification and subject recommendation tool for University of Cebu students. It checks your academic record against your programme's prospectus and explains every result, so planning your load stops being guesswork.

Features

  1. 1

    Prerequisite checking

    Every chain, year standing, and elective rule matched against your record.

  2. 2

    Explained results

    See the exact requirement behind every subject you can't take yet.

  3. 3

    Load planning

    A suggested set of subjects that fits within your allowable units.
