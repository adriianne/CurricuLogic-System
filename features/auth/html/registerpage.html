How registration works

Every registration is checked against the official EDP student list. If your details match, your account is approved at once.

  1. 1

    You submit a request

    New students provide their details. Students with an existing record only need their student ID.

  2. 2

    We check it against the EDP list

    Your details and your e-mail address are matched against the official EDP student list. Your program, year level, and prospectus are set from the list.

  3. 3

    You confirm your e-mail

    Open the link we send you, then sign in and see your subject eligibility for the coming term.

Use the e-mail address the EDP office has on file. Registrations that do not match the EDP student list are refused.
