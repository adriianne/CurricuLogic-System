Choosing a password

Passwords are never emailed. You get a one-time link, and you choose your own password on the page it opens.

  1. 1

    Enter your email

    The address your account was created with.

  2. 2

    Open the link

    It works once and expires, so open it soon after it arrives.

  3. 3

    Choose a new password

    Then log in with it as usual.
